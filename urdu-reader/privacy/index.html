Urdu for Hindi Readers Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy covers the iPhone app Urdu for Hindi Readers (shown on the Home Screen as “Urdu Reader”). It is made by Maaz Ahmad Shaikh, an independent developer in Canada (“I” below).

In short

  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
  • Your progress, settings and achievements are kept only on your iPhone
  • I don't receive, sell or share any personal data about you
  • The app doesn't connect to any server of mine. The only online feature is buying the full course, which is handled by Apple.

What stays on your iPhone

All of this is stored only inside the app on your iPhone. I can't see it.

  • Your progress: lessons finished, scores, XP, your streak and which words you know
  • Your achievements and the dates you earned them
  • Your settings: the app's language, daily goal, text size, sound, and the reminder time if you turn reminders on

Your iPhone's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include this data, as it does for most apps. That backup belongs to your account and is handled by Apple under its terms.

Audio

Words and sentences are read aloud by the speech voice built into iOS, on your iPhone. The app doesn't send the text anywhere to do this.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your iPhone. No push service is used, and nothing is sent to me. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your iPhone's Settings.

Purchases

The full course is a one-time purchase handled entirely by the App Store. I never receive your payment details. The app checks what you've bought on your iPhone, using Apple's StoreKit. Apple gives developers sales reports that don't identify you.

Links you open

If you tap the contact email, your email app opens and you choose whether to send anything. If you write to me, I use your email only to reply.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can erase your progress in the app (Me › Reset progress). Deleting the app from your iPhone deletes all of its data from the iPhone. Device backups follow your Apple backup settings. I don't hold any personal data about you, so there is nothing for me to delete.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA, the GDPR in the EU and UK, or US state laws), you may have the right to ask what personal data is held about you and to have it deleted. Because I don't collect any, there is normally nothing to provide, but you are welcome to ask.

Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, I'll update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app.
