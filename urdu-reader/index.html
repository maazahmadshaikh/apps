Coming soon to the App Store

If you speak Hindi, you already speak most of Urdu. What you're missing is the script. This course teaches it a few letters at a time, always through words you already know, and every Urdu word comes with its Devanagari spelling.

From letters to Ghalib

19 units in five stages: the alphabet, nasal sounds and hamza, then signs, forms, headlines, longer prose and classical couplets.

Learn by doing

Short lessons with many kinds of exercises: read the word, pick the spelling, build words from letters, match pairs and put sentences in order.

English or Hindi

The whole app and its guidebook are available in English and in Hindi. Switch any time.

Real print and poetry

Read newspaper headlines, shop signs and forms, and couplets by Mir, Ghalib, Zauq and Dagh, each checked against Rekhta.

Review that remembers

Words you miss come back in review until you know them. Daily goal, streaks, achievements and an optional reminder.

Made by a linguist

Written by Maaz Ahmad Shaikh, a linguist (PhD, University of Alberta) who translates between Hindi and Urdu.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your progress stays on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.

Price

The first stage is free. One purchase unlocks the whole course, with no subscription.
