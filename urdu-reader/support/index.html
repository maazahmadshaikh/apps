Common questions

How do I switch the app to Hindi?

Open Me › Settings › Language and choose हिन्दी. The lessons, guidebook and buttons all switch; the Urdu stays the same.

I don't hear any audio

Check that your iPhone isn't on silent and the volume is up, and that Sound is on in Me › Settings. The app uses your iPhone's built-in Hindi voice to read the Devanagari. If the voice sounds missing or very robotic, open iPhone Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › Hindi and download a voice.

Why does the audio read Hindi, not Urdu?

Urdu script leaves out most short vowels, so computer voices reading Urdu script often guess wrong (for example بہار read as Bihar instead of bahaar). The app has the voice read each word's Devanagari spelling instead, which is exact. A few sounds such as क़, ख़ and ग़ may come out flatter than a careful Urdu speaker would say them; the guidebook explains each one.

The Urdu text is too small or too large

Use the Urdu text size slider in Me › Settings.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Open Me and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

The app has no account. Your progress is included in your iPhone's backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), so restoring a backup to a new iPhone brings it back.

I'm not getting the daily reminder

Check that the reminder is on in Me › Settings, and that notifications are allowed for the app in iPhone Settings › Notifications. Focus or Do Not Disturb can also silence it. If you've already met your daily goal, that day's reminder is skipped.

Can I skip a stage I already know?

Yes. Each stage ends with a checkpoint. Open it from the path and pass it (80% or more) to unlock the next stage straight away.

Found a mistake in the course?

Every word, sentence and couplet has been checked, but if you spot an error in a spelling, a Devanagari form or a translation, please email me with the unit and the item, and I'll check and fix it.
