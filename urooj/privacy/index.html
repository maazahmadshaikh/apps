Urooj Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy covers Urooj on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac and Android. Urooj is made by Maaz Ahmad Shaikh, an independent developer in Canada (“I” below).

In short

  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
  • Prayer times, your location, your prayer tracker and all your settings are worked out and kept on your own devices
  • I don't receive, sell or share any personal data about you
  • Urooj only uses the internet for a few features you choose to use, listed below, and those requests go to the services named, not to me

What stays on your device

All of this is stored only in the app on your device. I can't see it.

  • Your location, used to calculate prayer times, the Qibla direction and the sun's position. You can type in a city instead of sharing your location.
  • Your settings, such as the calculation method, reminders, the wake-up alarm, language and appearance
  • Your prayer tracker, including prayers you've marked, fasts, excused days and make-up counts
  • Masjids and timetables you've saved or imported, and any adhan recordings you've added

On Apple devices, the iPhone app shares some of this with your own Apple Watch directly, and with Urooj's widgets on the same device, so they show the same times. Nothing in this passes through any server of mine.

Your device's own backup (iCloud Backup, or Google's Android backup) may include Urooj's data, as it does for most apps. That backup belongs to your account and is handled by Apple or Google under their terms. On Android, imported adhan recordings are left out of backups.

When Urooj uses the internet

Each of these happens only when you use the feature.

Place names and city search

To show the name of your area, or to find a city you type, Urooj sends the coordinates or search text to the system's geocoding service: Apple's on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and the one built into your Android device (usually provided by Google Play services).

Finding nearby masjids

  • iPhone, iPad and Mac: the search uses Apple Maps (MapKit) for the area shown.
  • Android: the area you're looking at is sent to OpenStreetMap's Overpass servers (overpass-api.de, with overpass.kumi.systems as a fallback) to list masjids, and map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers. Like any website, these servers receive your device's IP address.

Masjid timetable links

If you add a link to a masjid's calendar or timetable, Urooj downloads it from that address.

Reading timetables from photos and PDFs

When you import a timetable from a photo, scan or PDF, the text is read on your device: with Apple's Vision framework on Apple devices, and with Google's ML Kit on Android. The images and the text are not sent anywhere. Google's ML Kit sends Google anonymous metrics about how its text recognition performs and is used, and may download model updates; see Google's ML Kit terms. Android's document scanner is provided by Google Play services.

Community masjid times (iPhone, iPad and Mac)

If you choose to share a masjid's timetable, or to confirm or report one someone else shared, Urooj saves it in Urooj's public iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), where other Urooj users can see it. A shared timetable contains the masjid's name, its map location and identifier, and the prayer and iqamah times; it contains nothing about you. Confirmations and reports are stored with an anonymous iCloud identifier that Apple creates for Urooj, so that each person counts once; I can't see your name or your Apple Account from it. Sharing needs you to be signed in to iCloud and never happens unless you tap Share, Confirm or Report. To have a timetable you shared removed, email me.

Purchases

Supporter subscriptions, lifetime purchases and tips are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. I never receive your payment details. Urooj checks what you've bought on your device, using Apple's StoreKit or Google Play Billing. Apple and Google give developers sales reports that don't identify you.

Links you open

References in the guides (for example to Sunnah.com or Quran.com) open in your browser, under that website's own privacy policy.

Camera, notifications and permissions

  • Camera: used only while you use the sun path view (augmented reality) or scan a timetable. The live image stays on your device and isn't stored.
  • Notifications and alarms: prayer reminders and the Fajr wake-up alarm are scheduled on your device. No push service is used.
  • You can turn any permission off in your device's settings. Urooj keeps working; you can enter your city by hand.

Children

Urooj is suitable for all ages. It doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting Urooj from a device deletes its data from that device. Device backups follow Apple's or Google's backup settings. I don't hold any personal data about you, so there is nothing for me to delete, apart from any community timetable you shared, which I'll remove on request.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA, the GDPR in the EU and UK, or US state laws), you may have the right to ask what personal data is held about you and to have it deleted. Because I don't collect any, there is normally nothing to provide, but you are welcome to ask.

Changes

If Urooj starts handling data differently, I'll update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app.
