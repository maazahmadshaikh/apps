Common questions

My times differ from my masjid's

Masjids use different calculation methods and often add a few minutes for safety. In Settings, choose the method your masjid uses, and Hanafi or standard Asr. You can also add your masjid and its timetable so Urooj shows its iqamah times.

I don't hear the adhan or reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for Urooj in your device's settings, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them. On Android, also allow alarms and reminders, and turn off battery optimisation for Urooj. On Android, and on iPhone with iOS 26 or later, the Fajr wake-up alarm rings even when the phone is on silent; on earlier iOS versions it arrives as a notification.

Fajr and Isha look strange where I live in summer

Far from the equator, twilight can last all night in summer. Choose a high-latitude rule in Settings; the guide "Prayer at High Latitudes" in the app explains the options.

How do I restore my Supporter purchase?

Open Support Urooj (in More or Settings) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. Purchases made on Apple devices don't carry over to Android, and the other way round.

How do I cancel my Supporter subscription?

On iPhone, iPad or Mac: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play › Profile › Payments and subscriptions › Subscriptions. You keep the extras until the end of the year you paid for.

Is my prayer tracker backed up?

Urooj keeps your data on your device and has no account. It's included in your device's own backup (iCloud Backup or Google's Android backup), so restoring a backup to a new phone brings it back.

Is the money I give charity or zakat?

No. A Supporter purchase or tip is an optional gift to the developer for his work. It isn't charity and can't count as zakat.

Religious content

The guides set out the positions of the schools and scholars with their sources; they are not fatwas. If you find an error in a reference or a translation, please email me with the guide and the item, and I'll check it.
