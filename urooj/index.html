Urooj عروج

Prayer times for iPhone, Apple Watch, Mac and Android. Free, with no ads, ever.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

In Urdu, urooj means the zenith, the sun's highest point. Dhuhr begins at zawāl, when the sun passes that peak, so urooj-o-zawāl names the moment that defines a prayer time.

Accurate times, worked out on your device

All the major calculation methods, Hanafi or standard Asr, rules for high latitudes, and your masjid's iqamah times.

Reminders and a Fajr alarm

Adhan and reminders before each prayer, and a real wake-up alarm that moves with Fajr.

Qibla and the sun

A Qibla compass, and the sun's path across your sky, with an augmented-reality view.

Prayer tracker

Mark prayers and fasts, keep a streak, track make-up prayers, and mark excused days privately.

Guides with sources

Wudu and tayammum, the Sunnah of prayer, prayer while travelling and prayer at high latitudes, each school's view with checked references.

In 32 languages

Choose the app's language inside Urooj, whatever your phone is set to.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your location and your prayer data stay on your devices. Read the privacy policy.

Free, and supported by its users

Everything that helps you pray is free. If Urooj helps you, you can become a Supporter, or leave a tip, and get a few extras as thanks: the Living Sky background, colour themes, alternate app icons and extra widget designs.
